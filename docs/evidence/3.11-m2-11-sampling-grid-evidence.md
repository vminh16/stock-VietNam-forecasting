### 3.11 M2.11 Sampling Grid Evidence

A 2x2 factorial over `temperature` and `top_p` on the 98 M2.5 screen dates, read
against section 8.13. It was registered to separate a configuration cause from a
model cause for the calibration gap, and it separates them.

| cell | T | top_p | CRPS | coverage | width | RankIC |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `t06_p90` (incumbent) | 0.6 | 0.9 | 0.025553 | 0.398407 | 0.051589 | **0.031551** |
| `t06_p100` | 0.6 | 1.0 | 0.025189 | 0.456705 | 0.060847 | 0.027569 |
| `t10_p90` | 1.0 | 0.9 | **0.024977** | 0.532127 | 0.074703 | 0.010767 |
| `t10_p100` | 1.0 | 1.0 | 0.025338 | **0.587224** | 0.089957 | -0.001369 |

**No change is adopted.** Rule 1 names `t10_p90` on CRPS, and rule 3's guard
rejects it: its RankIC falls `0.020784` below the best cell against an allowance
of `0.005`. The rule as written has no fallback to the runner-up, so the
incumbent stands.

**The sampler explains 72% of the calibration gap.** Section 3.10 puts the
ceiling at `0.66`; the incumbent sits at `0.398`, and opening both dials reaches
`0.587`. Since `t10_p100` applies no filtering at all, that figure is the
model's undistorted predictive distribution, and it still falls `0.073` short.
**That residual, not the `0.262` this project had been quoting, is what a
fine-tune has to fix on calibration.** The two dials are close to additive on
coverage, with temperature about twice the lever truncation is.

**Opening the dials destroys the ranking signal.** RankIC runs `0.0316`,
`0.0276`, `0.0108`, `-0.0014` across the table, and the undistorted corner is
indistinguishable from zero with MW-DA at `47.03`, below a coin flip. The cause
is the sample count rather than the model: a ranking score uses the mean of the
sampled paths, that mean is estimated from ten draws, and widening the
distribution widens the error of the mean by the same factor. The narrow
incumbent setting does not describe the market better; it estimates a centre more
stably from very few draws. CRPS sees both effects, which is why its optimum is
interior.

Recorded as an observation and not as a decision: `t06_p100`, keeping
`temperature 0.6` and dropping the truncation, passes the guard at a RankIC drop
of `0.003982` while improving CRPS to `0.025189` and coverage to `0.456705`.
Adopting it requires its own registration and a confirmatory run on disjoint
dates, as section 8.13 requires of any winner.

The open question the grid raises is not which dial to turn but how many samples
to draw. Ten are enough to rank only because the distribution is held
artificially narrow. Sample count is part of a locked measurement, so testing it
needs its own registration.
