### 3.12 M2.12 Local Baseline Evidence

Read against section 8.14, registered in commit `ba9404b` before the run started.
One arm, `small_l126`, 13,431 origins on the 98 screen dates, 2,406 seconds at
5.58 origins per second on an RTX 2050.

#### The registered criterion is met, and the result is not what it appears

Rule 2 of section 8.14 allowed `|delta| <= 0.005230` on RankIC against the
`0.024733` of M2.5. The run returned `0.024732549764991712` against
`0.024732549764991712`. Every one of the seven per-date metric columns matches on
every one of the 98 dates at a maximum absolute difference of exactly `0.000e+00`.

A sampled forecast pipeline does not reproduce bitwise across two machines. It
reproduces bitwise when there is only one machine.

#### Three statements in section 8.14 are wrong

1. **"Every Kronos number this project holds was produced on a rented L4."**
   False. M2.5 and M2.7 are local.
2. **"no manifest field carries it"**, of the host. False. The `command` field

3. **"M2.5 and M2.11 ... ran the identical configuration on the identical dates

   `arm_id` **and** in host.

Section 8.14 is frozen and is not edited. The record of its defect is here.

#### What the run establishes, and what it leaves open

**Established.** The pipeline is fully deterministic on a fixed host. Fifteen
days, a different working tree and a different specification version apart,
13,431 origins reproduce at a difference of exactly zero. `seed`, `arm_id` and
host jointly determine the result, with nothing else entering it.

**Not established.** Anything about changing hosts. The run re-executed on the
machine that produced its own reference, so rule 2 was satisfied by a comparison
that could not test the hypothesis it was registered for. This is recorded as a
**vacuous pass**, not as evidence that the host is harmless.

**Now pointing the other way.** The `0.006818` gap between M2.5 and M2.11, which
this project has cited as 2.6 seed standard deviations, is confounded: those two
runs differ in host as well as in `arm_id`. It is the only pair of runs sharing a
configuration and a date set across the two machines, and it is wider than seed
noise alone predicts. The host may matter more than section 8.14 assumed, not
less.

#### What would test it

M2.9 ran `small_l126_r1` on the L4 over `{2, 5 mod 10}`. Re-running that exact
`arm_id` here holds the RNG stream, the dates, the sampler and the weights fixed
and varies only the machine. 26,862 origins at 5.58 per second is 1.34 hours.
That needs its own registration; section 8.14 is spent.

Until it is run, the rule stands unchanged and on its own merits: a local number
and an L4 number must never enter the same paired comparison.
