Read against section 8.15, registered in commit `c669cd1` before either arm was
computed. Two arms, 13,431 origins each on the 98 screen dates, run locally on

The last three rows are M2.5. Throughput is shown only where this machine
measured it at the same settings.

#### The registered outcome

`small_l40_norm126` scores `0.022144`, a difference of `-0.002588` from
`small_l126`, inside the `0.005230` band of rule 2. Under rule 4 this is the
first named outcome: **the context lever stays weak down to 40**, and the
section 12 premise that shorter contexts do not match `L=126` has screen
evidence against it. It runs at `13.20` origins per second against `5.58`, 2.4
times the throughput.

**Nothing is adopted.** These dates are spent on selection, and confirming the
finding needs dates disjoint from them, which the current date policy does not
have. The band itself is two seed standard deviations borrowed from the L4, so
its width is uncertain for the reason section 3.12 gives.

The exit item is discharged: the M2 exit list names "the `L=40` arm", and it has
been run and read.

#### What the match does not include

- **Calibration is not free.** Coverage falls from `0.4003` to `0.3738` and CRPS
  rises from `0.025549` to `0.025599`. These are diagnostics with no pass or fail
  under rule 3, but the shorter context produces a narrower predictive
  distribution, and it is the calibration side that section 3.11 says a
  fine-tune has to fix.
- **The folds disagree.** Against `small_l126`, `small_l40_norm126` is higher in
  2022 (`0.0272` against `0.0100`) and 2024 (`0.0287` against `0.0273`), and lower
  in 2023 (`0.0077` against `0.0294`) and 2025 (`0.0246` against `0.0328`). A
  pooled tie built from two wins and two losses of that size is a tie, not a
  demonstration of equivalence.

#### The normalizer series is not monotone

Matching the normalizer to the context gives `0.001251` at 63 and `0.011676` at
40, so the shorter context scores higher. Nothing in the design predicts that,
and with 98 dates and per-fold values that swing by `0.05` it is read as noise
around a low level, not as a finding. What the series does support, at all
three context lengths, is the M2.5 reading: a short normalizer is what costs
the ranking signal, and holding it at 126 recovers almost all of it.
